who's responsible

i'm ryana may que (schmayterling), and i run linkbed.me and linkbed.ryana.dev from the philippines. this policy covers those sites, while anyone hosting their own copy is responsible for theirs.

what i keep and why

i save your cards, who can edit them, and records that check your access token without storing the token itself. i also keep abuse reports and moderation decisions so i can deal with problems.

to prevent abuse, i use browser and network details, including your ip address, to recognise related activity and apply limits or bans automatically. if a restriction seems wrong, email me so i can review it.

i use this data to run the features you ask for, protect the service, and meet legal obligations, but i don't sell your personal data, use your cards for targeted ads, or add ad trackers.

cookies and saved settings

cookies keep you signed in and preserve guest edit access, and your browser also saves settings like your theme and recent emoji. it keeps unfinished edits and your current page in this tab so you can refresh without losing your place. signed-in sessions last up to 30 days, while guest access cookies last up to a year. you can clear or block this storage, but doing so may remove guest edit access or stop some features from working.

who else sees it

anyone with the link can view a published card, and discord or search engines may keep copies. if you load linked images or videos, or visit another site, those services also receive normal connection details.

vercel and cloudflare help run the service, which means they may handle data outside your country. i can also access records to maintain the site and review reports, and may share information when legally required or needed to protect people. if you donate, github handles payment without sharing your card details with me, and if you email me, my email provider handles that message.

how long it stays

cards stay until you or i delete them, while some access and moderation records have no fixed deletion date and are kept while needed for edit access, reports, or abuse prevention. even after deletion, data may remain in backups for about a week, and copies held by other services follow their own rules.

your choices

you can edit or delete your cards, and you can email hey@ryanaque.com with the relevant link to ask about your personal data. that includes requests to see, correct, export, or delete it, limit or object to its use, or withdraw consent where it applies. i may need to check it's you before acting on a request, but please don't send your access token.

if some records need to stay for legal reasons or abuse prevention, i'll explain why. you can also complain to your local privacy authority, including the philippine national privacy commission.

linkbed isn't intended for children under 13 or any higher minimum age where they live, so please tell me if a child has shared personal information here and i'll look into it.

if this changes

if this policy changes, i'll update the date here and post a notice for major changes. i'll also ask first if a new use needs your consent. for the rules on using linkbed, see the terms of service.
